Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Media Forge (MediaForge).

What is Media Forge (MediaForge)?

Media Forge is a 100% private, free online file converter. Unlike cloud converters that upload your confidential files to remote servers, Media Forge uses modern browser WebAssembly technologies to convert videos, audio, images, and documents directly on your device.

Why choose Media Forge over other converters?

With Media Forge, your files never leave your computer or phone. There are zero server queues, zero file size restrictions, and no tracking of your personal, business, or financial documents.

What formats does Media Forge support?

Media Forge supports video conversions (MP4, WebM, AVI, GIF), audio extraction and conversion (MP3, WAV, OGG, AAC), image resizing (JPG, PNG, WebP), and office spreadsheet/document conversions (Word DOCX, Excel XLSX, XLS, CSV, Markdown MD, TXT to PDF).
